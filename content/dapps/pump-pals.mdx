## About Pump Pals

Pump Pals is one of the seven launch-day applications on Fluent, focused on the consumer
gaming side of the ecosystem. The product turns fantasy sports performance into a reputation
primitive — your manager track record becomes data that other Fluent apps can read.

This is one of the clearer examples of Fluent's thesis in action: skill in one context
(fantasy sports forecasting) produces a verifiable signal that's useful in completely
different contexts (DeFi underwriting, social trust, predictor markets).
