## About Pump Pals

Pump Pals brings creator-driven collectible card games onchain. Players collect digital cards representing creators, trade them with other players, and battle for ranking and rewards.

The game runs natively on Fluent, taking advantage of the blended execution environment to combine fluid trading mechanics with battle logic that would be expensive on a single-VM chain.

## Why it matters

Most creator economies are platform-locked (TikTok coins, Twitch bits, OnlyFans tips). Pump Pals makes creator engagement a tradable, ownable, composable on-chain asset — and adds a gaming layer that gives the asset functional utility beyond speculation.
