## About Fluent Connect & Prints

Fluent Connect is the user-facing product for Prints, the reputation layer Fluent launched
in January 2026 — three months before mainnet. It aggregates behavioral signals from
multiple sources into a single programmable profile:

- On-chain activity across Fluent and connected wallets
- Ethos scores (a separate decentralized reputation protocol)
- Kaito smart-follower data (social signal quality)
- Predictor accuracy from apps like Pulse Predictor

What makes this different from past on-chain identity attempts is that Prints isn't a
single score — it's a multi-dimensional reputation primitive. A wallet can be a top-tier
DeFi user, a mid-tier predictor, and a low-tier social participant simultaneously. Each
context surfaces the dimension that matters to it.

For builders, Fluent's pitch is "integrate Prints to add reputation to your app" — meaning
any dApp can read these signals and gate access, pricing, or rewards based on them. This
is what turns Prints from a vanity profile into infrastructure.

This is the single most strategically important listing in this directory, because almost
every other Fluent dApp depends on it.
