## About Blend

Blend is the yield aggregation tool that launched alongside Fluent's mainnet. Where most
yield aggregators are constrained to a single VM, Blend takes advantage of Fluent's blended
execution to compose strategies across both EVM and Wasm protocols within a single
atomic position.

This is the kind of product that's *only possible* on Fluent — single-VM chains can't
offer it. That makes Blend a useful demonstration case for what blended execution
unlocks in practice.

Note: Blend (the protocol) is distinct from BLEND (the Fluent network token). The protocol
name predates the token ticker.
