## About Yumi

Yumi was one of the most-watched launches on Fluent because it tackles the on-chain credit problem
head-on. Pure crypto lending has been stuck at over-collateralized models — meaning you can only
borrow if you already have more than you want to borrow.

Yumi flips the model. By using Prints as a credit signal, it offers genuine BNPL functionality
without requiring upfront collateral, betting on the user's reputation as the underwriting basis.

The protocol is one of the clearest demonstrations of why reputation-aware infrastructure matters
for the next phase of DeFi.
