## About Prints

Prints is Fluent's reputation primitive — the chain-native answer to "who is this wallet, and what have they done?" Originally part of Fluent Connect, Prints now stands on its own with a dedicated brand and account at [@printsxyz](https://x.com/printsxyz).

Reputation is built across four families: Identity (verification + KYC signals), Tester (early-network participation), Builder (smart contract deployments and dev activity), and Influential (on-chain reach and engagement). Each family scores independently.

## Why this matters

Most chains treat all wallets as anonymous and identical. Prints lets capital — and apps — distinguish between a brand-new wallet and someone who has earned trust over time. Vena uses it for borrowing rates. Flux uses it for trading-fee boosts. Yumi will use it for unsecured credit underwriting. The same reputation signal powers each.
