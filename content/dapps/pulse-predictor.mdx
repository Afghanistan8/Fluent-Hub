## About Pulse

Pulse is an onchain prediction polling platform on Fluent. Users vote on polls across categories — politics, crypto, sports, technology, culture, finance, AI, economy, science — and build a reputation score based on how accurate their predictions are.

The platform uses Privy for authentication, which connects directly with the Fluent Connect/Prints reputation system. As you make correct calls over time, your social capital increases — turning forecasting accuracy into a verifiable on-chain track record.

## Why it matters

Most prediction markets focus on price discovery via betting. Pulse focuses on reputation discovery via consensus accuracy. Correctly forecasting trends becomes a portable signal you carry into other Fluent applications.
