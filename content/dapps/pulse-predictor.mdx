## About Pulse Predictor

Pulse Predictor turns prediction accuracy into reputation. Every call you make is logged on-chain,
and the protocol feeds your aggregate accuracy back into Prints as a "predictor score" — one of
the contextual reputation signals Fluent is building out.

Unlike traditional prediction markets which optimize purely for liquidity, Pulse is designed to
create a portable, verifiable reputation primitive: a wallet that consistently predicts well
becomes valuable to other protocols that want to surface high-signal community members.
