## About Flux

Flux is the native concentrated-liquidity DEX on Fluent, designed specifically for the BlendedVM architecture. Unlike typical DEXs that handle a single token standard, Flux pools support both ERC20 (Ethereum-style) and SPL20 (Solana-style) tokens in the same pool — eliminating the cross-VM bridging that fragmented liquidity for years.

The protocol uses concentrated liquidity (à la Uniswap v3) with flexible fee tiers: 0.01% for stablecoin pairs, up to 1% for memecoin pairs. Just-in-time liquidity provisioning means capital is available exactly when trades need it.

## Why reputation matters here

Flux integrates [Prints](/dapps/prints) — Fluent's reputation primitive — for sybil resistance and boosted yields. Verified humans earn 1.5x points on every trade; unverified addresses earn the standard 1.0x. The thesis: real humans should earn more than farming bots.
