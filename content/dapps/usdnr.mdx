## About USDnr

USDnr is Fluent's native stablecoin, launched on day one of mainnet with $50 million in
committed liquidity. It's built on M0 infrastructure through partner Nerona, with the
underlying reserves held in U.S. Treasury bills.

The T-bill yield generated from those reserves accrues to the protocol rather than to
individual holders directly, which positions USDnr differently from yield-bearing
stablecoins like sDAI or USDe. The thesis is that protocol-level yield funds ecosystem
growth, which in turn drives demand for the stablecoin itself.

For users, USDnr is the default base asset on Fluent DeFi — most lending markets,
yield strategies, and DEX pairs are denominated in it.
